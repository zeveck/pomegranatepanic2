# Pomegranate Panic 2 — brief

## In one sentence

A runaway pomegranate tumbles down through the Greek underworld while you slap
marble ramps under it to keep it out of Hades' fire, and if you gather all six
of Persephone's seeds, Hades himself rises to stop you and you bounce off his
face until he breaks and Persephone goes free.

**Feeling:** frantic, funny arcade panic that keeps getting faster, then a
big, loud, triumphant boss moment that feels earned.

## Who, where, how long

Anyone with a phone or a browser. Phones in portrait (one-thumb tapping, like
the original, which is mobile-first) and desktop with a mouse. Runs last 1–4
minutes; a full run that reaches and beats Hades is roughly 3–5 minutes. Built
for "one more go".

## Style references

- **Pomegranate Panic (the original, same author's project, used with their
  blessing as the basis):** borrow everything about the core: portrait
  endless descent, random ramp pieces with a NEXT preview (Tetris-like),
  rolling physics, big-drop bounces that chain into combos, round pinball
  bumpers incl. a multi-hit golden jackpot, Hades' fire at the bottom as the
  death zone, six seeds that each speed you up and pay out more, golden pom.
  Avoid: its flat navy-and-brown look and its sparse sound.
- **Greek myth (public domain):** Hades, Persephone, the six pomegranate seeds
  that bound her to the underworld. Our own designs for both: no likeness
  from any film or game (in particular, no blue flaming-hair Hades).
- **Look to aim for:** a rich, painted storybook take on Greek pottery and
  marble: deep indigo/violet caverns, ember orange fire, crimson pomegranate,
  gold trim. Readable at phone size.

## Settings and decisions made for them

- Platform both, web target, medium scope, $20 budget, 2 review rounds (all
  defaults).
- The game is built fresh (not a patch of the original's code), but it keeps
  the original's rules and feel and tunes from its numbers.
- Seeds keep appearing until you hold six (the original stopped after six had
  appeared), so the Hades fight is reachable by a good player, not just a
  lucky one.
